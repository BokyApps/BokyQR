BokyQR 0.1.0 (versionCode 1)

* Scan QR codes with the camera, or import one from the photo picker.
* Show every scanned payload as text first. Only an http/https URL that passes the full payload
  policy, and a passkey QR, are ever offered for opening, and only on a tap.
* Never open intent:, javascript:, file:, content:, android-app:, tel:, mailto: and the rest.
* Refuse to open localhost, private, link-local and cloud metadata addresses; show them as text.
* Optional VirusTotal, urlscan.io and URLhaus reputation checks, each behind its own disclosure,
  each using a key you supply. No key ships with the app.
* F-Droid build: ZXing only. No ML Kit, no Play Services, no Firebase, no analytics.
