BokyQR 0.1.0 (versionCode 1)

* Scan QR codes with the camera or from the photo picker.
* Show every payload as text first; only a URL that passes the policy, and a passkey QR, are ever
  opened, and only on a tap.
* Never open intent:, javascript:, file:, content:, tel:, mailto: or the rest.
* Never open localhost, private or link-local addresses, or cloud metadata endpoints.
* Optional VirusTotal, urlscan.io and URLhaus checks, each disclosed, each on your own key.
